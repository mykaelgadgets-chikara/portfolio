Weekly Intelligence Agent — for an oil & gas market entrant

Context-specific prompt engineering · client engagement · designed, delivered & taught
Client work

For a Nigerian company entering the oil & gas sector, I built a production-grade "weekly intelligence agent" — a master system prompt that turns a general AI model into a board-level domain consultant, producing a weekly market-intelligence briefing and a one-page executive summary (Word + PDF) — then trained the client to run it themselves.

  • Engineered for rigour, not fluency: source credibility-tiering, mandatory dated citations, confidence scoring, explicit assumption-tagging, and a hard never-fabricate rule.
  • Built-in fraud screening: every opportunity screened against known scam patterns and classified verified / plausible / unverified / likely-scam — because that market is a high-fraud zone.
  • Tailored to the client's real edge: a bespoke strategic annex mapping their existing business into specific regulators, funds and lawful entry routes — not generic advice.
  • Versioned, with weekly continuity — a change-log and a live-opportunity tracker — handed over as a system the client operates independently.
